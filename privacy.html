隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 19 日

一、适用范围

南通睦融电气设备有限公司（以下简称“睦融电气”或“我们”）重视您的个人信息。本政策说明您访问本网站、向我们咨询或与我们联系时，我们可能如何处理相关信息。

二、我们可能收集的信息

  • 您主动提供的信息：姓名、公司名称、电话、邮箱、船舶信息、故障描述以及您在邮件、电话或其他沟通中提供的内容。
  • 网站运行信息：服务器可能为安全和故障排查记录 IP 地址、浏览器类型、访问时间、请求页面及错误日志。
  • 第三方资源请求信息：网页加载百度地图、Tailwind CDN，以及由 Unsplash、Wikimedia 托管的图片或地图资源时，您的浏览器会直接连接相应服务。相关服务可能接收 IP 地址、浏览器与设备信息、请求网址、来源页面及访问时间，用于传输资源、安全防护和故障排查，并按照各自的隐私规则处理。
  • 语言偏好：网站使用浏览器本地存储保存中文或英文选择，键名为 murong_lang。该功能不用于跨站跟踪。

三、处理目的与依据

我们仅在业务所需或法律允许的范围内使用信息，用于回复咨询、评估服务需求、准备报价与技术方案、履行合同、维护网站安全、改进服务以及履行法定义务。需要征得同意的场景，我们会按适用法律取得同意。

四、共享与披露

我们不会出售您的个人信息。网站会为呈现页面和地图而请求百度、Tailwind CDN、Unsplash 和 Wikimedia 的资源；访问这些资源产生的信息由相应提供方直接接收。为完成服务，我们还可能在必要范围内向受保密义务约束的技术服务商、物流或项目合作方提供信息；也可能依据法律法规、监管要求或有效司法程序进行披露。我们会依据适用法律确认所需的告知、同意或其他处理依据；涉及跨境服务时，将采取相应保护措施。

五、保存期限与安全

我们仅在实现上述目的、履行合同或满足法定保存要求所需的期限内保留信息，期满后将删除或匿名化。我们采用合理的管理和技术措施保护信息，但互联网传输和电子存储无法保证绝对安全，请勿通过公开表单发送密码、支付凭证或与服务无关的敏感信息。

六、您的权利

在适用法律规定的范围内，您可以请求查询、更正、补充或删除您的个人信息，撤回同意，或对相关处理提出说明和异议。我们可能需要核验您的身份，并在法律规定的期限内处理请求。撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意进行的处理。

七、未成年人

本网站面向企业客户和专业服务需求，不以未成年人为目标。若您认为未成年人未经适当授权向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

八、政策更新

我们可能因业务、技术或法律变化更新本政策。重要变化会通过本页面发布，并更新生效日期。建议您定期查看本页面。

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

1. Scope

Nantong Murong Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. (“Murong Electrical”, “we”, or “us”) values your privacy. This Policy explains how we may handle information when you visit this website, make an enquiry, or contact us.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Information you provide: name, company, telephone number, email address, vessel details, fault descriptions, and information included in emails, calls, or other communications.
  • Operational data: hosting systems may record IP address, browser type, access time, requested pages, and error logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Third-party resource request data: when the page loads Baidu Maps, Tailwind CDN, and images or map resources hosted by Unsplash or Wikimedia, your browser connects directly to those services. They may receive IP address, browser and device details, requested URL, referring page, and access time to deliver resources, protect their services, and troubleshoot errors under their own privacy terms.
  • Language preference: the website stores your Chinese or English choice locally in your browser under murong_lang. It is not used for cross-site tracking.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

We use information only as needed or permitted by law to answer enquiries, assess service requirements, prepare quotations and technical proposals, perform contracts, secure and improve the website, and meet legal obligations. Where consent is required, we will request it as required by applicable law.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. The website requests resources from Baidu, Tailwind CDN, Unsplash, and Wikimedia to display pages and maps; information generated by those requests is received directly by the relevant provider. Where necessary to deliver services, we may also share limited information with service providers, logistics providers, or project partners subject to confidentiality obligations. We assess the notice, consent, or other lawful basis required by applicable law and take appropriate safeguards for cross-border services.

5. Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as needed for the stated purposes, contract performance, or legal retention requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, or unrelated sensitive information through public forms.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, completion, or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent, or ask for an explanation and object to certain processing. We may verify your identity and will respond within the legally required period. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

7. Children

This website is intended for business customers and professional service enquiries, not children. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Policy to reflect business, technical, or legal changes. Material changes will be published here with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.

9. Contact

To exercise privacy rights or ask a question, email hmm@rongelec.com, call 15682112719, or contact Nantong Murong Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd., Wujie Town, Tongzhou District, Nantong, Jiangsu, China.
